SlideFlow

Local-first • No database • HTML templates

Build and play HTML slide decks in minutes.

SlideFlow lets you assemble HTML slides, reorder them with drag and drop, then run a clean full-screen presentation. Everything stays in your browser using localStorage.

What you can do

  • Choose the number of slides (cover included).
  • Drop your HTML files or pick from templates.
  • Reorder with drag and drop.
  • Play with arrows + zoom in/out.
