Strengths

  • Clear planning

    Dates, budget, and scope defined from the start.

  • Strong documentation

    Ideal for audits and knowledge transfer.

  • Structured discipline

    Each phase has clear, validated deliverables.

  • Regulatory compliance

    Suitable for highly regulated industries (medical, aerospace).

Limitations

  • Low flexibility

    Hard to change direction once the project starts.

  • Tunnel effect

    The customer sees the final product only at the end.

  • Late risk discovery

    Integration issues often appear late.

  • Cost of change

    Changing requirements during testing is expensive.

Best for projects with a stable, well-defined scope.
