Agile Method (Scrum)

Iterative, incremental, and collaborative approach

03

Key Principles

Short Iterations

2–4 week sprints to deliver regularly.

Customer Value

Continuous prioritization by business value.

Self-organization

Cross-functional, autonomous teams.

Continuous Improvement

Inspect and adapt at every retrospective.

The Scrum Cycle

Strengths

Adaptability

Change is welcomed, even late in development.

Faster time-to-market

Rapid delivery of an MVP (Minimum Viable Product).

Quality & Risk

Faster bug detection and immediate user feedback.

Limitations

Team maturity

Requires an experienced, self-disciplined team.

Budget uncertainty

Final cost and exact scope are hard to guarantee upfront.

Scope drift risk

Without a strong Product Owner, scope can expand endlessly.

Best for innovative projects where needs evolve.
