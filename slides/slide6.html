When to use each method?

Decision matrix and typical use cases

05

Waterfall

Stability & Predictability

Best choice if:

Requirements are stable and complete upfront (few changes expected).

Strict regulatory or contractual constraints (e.g., medical, aerospace).

Heavy hardware dependencies or physical infrastructure.

Technologies are fully mastered with low technical uncertainty.

Keywords: rigor, audit, compliance

Agile

Adaptability & Speed

Best choice if:

Needs are evolving or unclear, with high initial uncertainty.

Priority on time-to-market and fast learning.

High user interaction (UX) needed to refine the product.

Produits digitaux, logiciels SaaS et innovation.

Keywords: value, feedback, innovation

Hybrid

The best of both

Examples of use:

Hardware + Software: V-model for hardware, Scrum for embedded software.

Water-Scrum-Fall: classic framing and deployment, Agile delivery.

At scale (SAFe): structured framework to coordinate multiple Agile teams.

Keywords: pragmatic, transition, scale

Tip: Don't choose a method because it's trendy, choose it to solve a specific problem.
